## Sintese final da matrix

### Onde Intercom domina (12 features)
1. AI sophistication (Fin Apex 1.0 publicado, custom actions, simulation, RAG patentes)
2. In-app Messenger SDK best-in-class
3. Marketplace formal 300+ apps
4. Reporting & Analytics polished
5. Pre-built reports
6. Custom reports via add-on
7. Compliance certs out-of-box (SOC2/ISO/HIPAA)
8. Multibrand
9. Product Tours
10. In-app outbound messages polished
11. G2 reviews count + market share
12. GraphQL API

### Onde Chatwoot domina (18 features)
1. Free tier permanente + self-hosted unlimited
2. Entry pricing ($0 vs $29)
3. Mid-tier pricing 50-65% mais barato
4. SLAs por 70% menos preco
5. Telegram nativo
6. TikTok nativo
7. Line nativo
8. API Channels custom
9. WhatsApp BYOC sem markup
10. Outbound WhatsApp included
11. Source code MIT modificavel
12. Self-hosting / data sovereignty
13. Multilingual help center em tier inferior
14. CSAT included em mid-tier
15. Round-robin desde Hacker
16. Multiple inboxes desde Hacker
17. Smart FAQs explicit feature
18. AI cost predictability (credits)

### Veredito da matrix

**Para SMB (5 agents) sensivel a custo + WhatsApp-first + Telegram/TikTok presence:** Chatwoot wins por margem larga.

**Para SaaS B2B mid-market (20-50 agents) com volume alto, in-app heavy, regulatorio enterprise:** Intercom wins se TCO sustenta.

**Para mid-market generico (20 agents) sem regulatorio especial:** Chatwoot Cloud Business + Captain credits oferece economia 50-65% vs Intercom Advanced + Fin com volume real ainda nao validado em benchmarks publicos.

A tensao real esta em **C1 (AI deflection performance)** — se Captain entrega mesmo deflection que Fin para um caso de uso especifico, Chatwoot domina absoluto. Se Captain entrega 30% e Fin 60%, math muda completamente.
