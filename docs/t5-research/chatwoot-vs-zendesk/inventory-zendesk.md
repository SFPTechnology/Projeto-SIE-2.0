# Inventory — Zendesk

**Snapshot:** 2026-05-06
**Versao avaliada:** Zendesk Suite 2026 (Support Team / Suite Team / Growth / Professional / Enterprise)
**Categoria:** SaaS Enterprise omnichannel customer service
**License:** Proprietary (no self-hosted option)
**HQ:** San Francisco, USA
**Fundado:** 2007 (~19 anos de maturidade)

## TL;DR

Zendesk e o lider global de market share (14.8% na categoria customer experience), com 6.000+ G2 reviews, 1.977 apps no marketplace, e a posicao de compliance mais comprehensiva do segmento (SOC2, ISO 27001/27018/27701, FedRAMP, HIPAA BAA, GDPR BCR). Suite Team comeca em $55/agent/mo (USD anual). Add-ons sao expressivos: Copilot $50, QA $35, WFM $25, ADPP $50. **TCO real 2-3x preco base** apos extensoes necessarias. AI fragmentado pos-aquisicao Ultimate.ai. Sem self-hosted = vendor lock-in. BR-fit decente: pricing BRL publico + suporte PT-BR + sales BR.

## 11. Diferenciais unicos

1. **Lider de market share** 14.8% (12.77% Intercom segundo, depois Trustpilot 12.26%)
2. **Marketplace 1.977 apps** — second-largest cloud marketplace SaaS
3. **Compliance posture mais extensa** do segmento: SOC2, ISO 27001/27018/27701, FedRAMP, HIPAA BAA, GDPR BCR
4. **Multi-region data residency**: US, EU/Dublin, AU, JP
5. **G2 6.000+ reviews** — maior universo de feedback validado
6. **AI Agents post-Ultimate.ai** com customers reportando 39-92% improvements
7. **Voice/Talk first-class** (nao add-on)
8. **Sandbox environment** Enterprise
9. **BR-fit decente:** BRL publico + suporte PT-BR + sales BR
10. **Apple Business Chat nativo** (raro)

## 12. Weaknesses conhecidas

1. **TCO real 2-3x base price** apos add-ons necessarios
2. Sticker $169 Enterprise vs $99 Chatwoot Enterprise = 1.7x
3. **AI fragmentado:** pos-Ultimate.ai + Copilot + AI Agents = semantica complexa
4. **AI Agents per-resolution pricing nao publico**
5. Data Center Location addon Pro/Enterprise REQUER ativacao manual (default = data movel sem aviso)
6. **Per-tier jumps:** Team $55 → Growth $89 (+62%); Growth → Pro $115 (+29%); Pro → Enterprise $169 (+47%)
7. **Vendr median deal $47.772/year** — sinal de cost escalation
8. **Self-hosted impossivel** (vendor lock-in)
9. **Switching pain** documentado em Hiver/Featurebase/Helpjuice
10. Pricing BRL R$359-1.105 inviabiliza PME mesmo com BR-fit razoavel
