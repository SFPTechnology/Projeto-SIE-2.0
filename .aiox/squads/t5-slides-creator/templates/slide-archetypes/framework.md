## FRAMEWORK TYPES & SPECIFICATIONS

### TYPE 1: 2×2 MATRIX (BCG Matrix, Ansoff, Eisenhower, etc.)

**Quando usar:**
- Segmentação em 4 categorias
- 2 dimensões de avaliação
- Portfolio analysis

**Especificações da estrutura:**

**Dimensions:**
- Width: 18cm (colunas 1.5-11)
- Height: 12cm (linhas 2-6.5)
- Center: Intersection at 50/50 (unless asymmetric by design)

**Axes:**
- Horizontal axis: 3pt line, #003B5C
- Vertical axis: 3pt line, #003B5C
- Arrows: 12pt arrowheads em ambos os ends

**Axis labels:**
- X-axis: Bottom (left label) e Top (right label)
  - Example: "Low ← Market Attractiveness → High"
  - Font: 12pt Bold, #000000
  - Position: Centered horizontalmente, 0.5cm from axis

- Y-axis: Left (bottom label) e Right (top label)
  - Example: "Weak ↓ Competitive Position ↑ Strong"
  - Font: 12pt Bold, #000000
  - Rotation: 90° (vertical)
  - Position: Centered verticalmente, 0.5cm from axis

**Quadrant backgrounds:**
- **Option A (subtle):** Cada quadrante cor diferente, 15% opacity
  - Top-right (best): #E8F5E9 (light green)
  - Top-left: #FFF9E6 (light yellow)
  - Bottom-right: #FFEBEE (light red)
  - Bottom-left: #F5F5F5 (light gray)

- **Option B (clean):** Sem background colors, apenas borders
  - Dividing lines: 1pt, #E0E0E0

**Quadrant labels:**
- Position: Canto superior de cada quadrante
- Font: 14pt Bold, #6E6E6E
- Examples: "Stars", "Cash Cows", "Question Marks", "Dogs"

**Data points (bubbles):**
- Size: Proporcional a 3ª dimensão (ex: revenue)
- Color: By category ou by quadrant
- Border: 2pt white (para destacar)
- Opacity: 85-90%

**Labels dos pontos:**
- Inside bubble (se couber): 10pt Bold, white ou black (depends on contrast)
- Outside com connector line (se não couber): 9pt Regular, background white 80%

**Legend:**
- Position: Bottom-right, fora do matrix
- Format:
  - Bubble sizes: "○ $100M | ◉ $500M | ⬤ $1B+"
  - Colors: Square + Label (se by category)

**Exemplo completo:**

**2×2 MATRIX - BCG Growth-Share para Portfolio de 8 Produtos**

**Axes:**
- X-axis: "Low (5%) ← Market Growth Rate → High (25%)"
- Y-axis: "Low (5%) ← Relative Market Share → High (3×)"
- Intersection: 10% growth (horizontal), 1× share (vertical)

**Quadrants:**
- Top-right: "Stars" (#E8F5E9 background, 15% opacity)
- Top-left: "Question Marks" (#FFF9E6 background)
- Bottom-right: "Cash Cows" (#E3F2FD background)
- Bottom-left: "Dogs" (#FFEBEE background)

**Bubbles:**
- Product A: Top-right (Star), 15% growth, 2.1× share, bubble Ø80px (revenue $450M)
- Product B: Top-right (Star), 18% growth, 1.8× share, bubble Ø65px ($320M)
- Product C: Bottom-right (Cash Cow), 6% growth, 2.5× share, bubble Ø90px ($580M)
- Product D-H: Positioned accordingly
- Colors: All #003B5C with 85% opacity
- Labels: Product names inside bubbles, white text 10pt Bold

**Legend:**
- Bubble size guide: ○ $100M | ◉ $300M | ⬤ $500M+
- Position: Linha 7, Colunas 9-12

**Annotations:**
- Arrow: De "Question Mark X" para "Star" quadrant, 2pt #00A86B, dashed
- Label: "Strategic move: Invest to gain share" - 9pt Italic

### TYPE 2: PYRAMID (Hierarchy, Maslow, Pyramid Principle)

**Quando usar:**
- Hierarquias
- Frameworks building blocks
- Priorização em níveis

**Especificações:**

**Pyramid structure:**
- Levels: 3-5 (ideal: 4)
- Width progression: Cada nível 15-20% mais estreito que anterior
- Height: Níveis iguais OU base maior (emphasize foundation)

**Dimensions:**
- Total width (base): 16cm
- Total height: 10-12cm
- Center-aligned horizontalmente

**Colors:**
- Gradient top-to-bottom (subtle)
- OR solid colors diferentes por nível
- Borders: 2pt white (separar níveis)

**Level labels:**
- Inside cada nível: 12-14pt Bold, color com contrast >4.5:1
- Format: "Level name" + "Key descriptor"

**Example - Pyramid Principle:**

**Level 1 (Top):** "Main Answer/Conclusion"
- Width: 6cm, height: 2cm
- Color: #003B5C
- Text: 14pt Bold, #FFFFFF, center

**Level 2:** "3 Supporting Arguments"
- Width: 10cm, height: 2.5cm
- Color: #00A86B (lighter than top)
- Text: 12pt Bold, #FFFFFF
- Split em 3 sections com dividers

**Level 3:** "9 Supporting Facts"
- Width: 14cm, height: 2.5cm
- Color: #81C784 (lighter ainda)
- Text: 10pt Regular, #000000
- Split em 9 small sections

**Level 4 (Base):** "Data & Evidence"
- Width: 16cm, height: 3cm
- Color: #C8E6C9 (lightest)
- Text: 10pt Regular, #000000

**Side annotations:**
- Brace/bracket indicando groupings
- Labels: "Strategic", "Tactical", "Operational" (dependendo do framework)

### TYPE 3: PROCESS FLOW (Linear, Cyclical)

**Quando usar:**
- Processos sequenciais
- Customer journeys
- Implementation roadmaps
- Continuous improvement cycles

**Especificações LINEAR:**

**Boxes:**
- Quantity: 4-7 steps (ideal: 5)
- Size: 3cm × 2cm cada (uniform)
- Spacing: 1cm entre boxes
- Alignment: Horizontal, center-aligned verticalmente

**Arrows:**
- Width: 2pt
- Color: #003B5C
- Style: Solid line com arrowhead
- Size: Arrowhead 8-10pt

**Step boxes:**
- Border: 2pt, cor matching step status
  - Completed: #00A86B (green)
  - In progress: #FF6F00 (orange)
  - Not started: #B0BEC5 (gray)
- Background: White OR 10% opacity da border color
- Border radius: 6px (rounded corners)

**Step labels:**
- Number: Circle no topo do box, 24px Ø, background matching border color
  - Text: "1", "2", "3"... em white, 12pt Bold
- Title: 12pt Bold, #000000, inside box, top area
- Description: 10pt Regular, #6E6E6E, inside box, bottom area (2-3 linhas max)

**Timeline (opcional):**
- Below boxes: Horizontal line com time markers
- Format: "Week 1-4", "Month 2", "Q3 2025"
- Font: 9pt Regular, #6E6E6E

**Example - 5 Step Process:**

**Step 1: Diagnose**
- Box: 3×2cm, border 2pt #00A86B (completed), radius 6px
- Number: Circle "1", green background
- Title: "Diagnose" - 12pt Bold
- Description: "Assess current state via interviews + data analysis" - 10pt
- Time: "Weeks 1-2" below

**Steps 2-5:** Similar structure, progressively different status colors

**Arrows:** Connecting each box, 2pt #003B5C, 1cm length

---

**Especificações CYCLICAL (circular/continuous):**

**Circle structure:**
- Diameter: 12-14cm
- Center: Middle do slide
- Segments: 4-6 (ideally divisível por círculo)

**Segments:**
- Equal angles (ex: 4 segments = 90° cada)
- Curved borders separating: 2pt white
- Colors: Alternating shades ou sequential gradient
- Direction: Clockwise (standard)

**Arrows (circular):**
- Curved arrows indicando direction
- Width: 3pt
- Color: #003B5C
- Position: Between segments (outside circle)

**Segment labels:**
- Outside: 12pt Bold, #000000, radial alignment
- Inside: Ícone + brief descriptor, 10pt Regular

**Center label:**
- Circle: 3cm Ø
- Content: Core concept ("Continuous Improvement", "Customer-Centric")
- Font: 14pt Bold, #003B5C

**Example - PDCA Cycle:**

4 segments (Plan, Do, Check, Act), 90° each, clockwise
- Segment colors: #E3F2FD, #C5E1A5, #FFF9C4, #FFCCBC
- Center: "PDCA Cycle" em 3cm circle
- Arrows: Curved, between segments, clockwise
- Each segment: Ícone + "Plan: Define objectives" (etc)

### TYPE 4: PILLARS / BUILDING BLOCKS

**Quando usar:**
- Strategic pillars
- Foundation + components
- Multi-dimensional frameworks

**Especificações:**

**Base/Foundation:**
- Width: Full width (20cm)
- Height: 1.5-2cm
- Color: #003B5C (navy - strong foundation)
- Label: "Foundation: [Core principle]" - 13pt Bold, #FFFFFF

**Pillars:**
- Quantity: 3-5 (ideal: 4)
- Width: 3-4cm cada
- Height: 6-8cm
- Spacing: 1-1.5cm entre pillars
- Top-aligned no foundation

**Pillar design:**
- Shape: Rectangle com top ligeiramente mais largo (architectural pillar)
- Color: Unique per pillar (#00A86B, #1976D2, #FF6F00, #7E57C2)
- Border: None ou 2pt darker shade
- Gradient: Subtle vertical (darker bottom → lighter top)

**Pillar labels:**
- Title: Inside, top area, 14pt Bold, #FFFFFF
- Sub-bullets: 2-4 items, 10pt Regular, #FFFFFF, indented
- Icon: Top de cada pillar, 32×32px, white

**Roof/Top element (opcional):**
- Triangular top conectando pillars
- Label: "Vision: [Ultimate goal]"
- Color: Lighter shade, #78909C

**Example - 4 Pillars of Strategy:**

**Foundation:**
- "Customer-Centricity as Core Principle"
- Full width, 2cm height, #003B5C

**Pillar 1: Customer Experience**
- 3.5×7cm, #00A86B
- Icon: User (32px, white)
- Title: "Customer Experience"
- Bullets: "• NPS >70", "• <2h response time", "• Omnichannel"

**Pillars 2-4:** Similar, different colors e content

**Roof:**
- Triangle spanning all 4 pillars
- "Vision: Market Leader by 2027"
- #B0BEC5, 12pt Bold

### TYPE 5: FUNNEL (Conversion, Sales Pipeline)

**Quando usar:**
- Conversion processes
- Sales funnels
- Customer acquisition journeys

**Especificações:**

**Funnel shape:**
- Trapezoids stacked, progressively narrower
- Stages: 4-6 (ex: Awareness → Interest → Consideration → Purchase → Loyalty)
- Total width (top): 18cm
- Total width (bottom): 6cm
- Total height: 12cm

**Stage design:**
- Height: Equal OU proportional ao volume (wider stages taller)
- Colors: Gradient top-to-bottom (#E3F2FD → #003B5C)
- Borders: 2pt white separating stages
- Alignment: Center-aligned

**Labels (inside cada stage):**
- Stage name: 12pt Bold, color com contrast
- Volume: 14pt Bold (ex: "10,000 leads")
- Conversion rate: 11pt Regular (ex: "↓ 40% convert")

**Side metrics:**
- Right side: Percentages ou absolute numbers
- Left side: Drop-off rates
- Font: 10pt Regular, #6E6E6E

**Annotations:**
- Arrows pointing to leaks/issues
- Boxes com improvement opportunities

**Example - Sales Funnel:**

**Stage 1 (Top): Awareness**
- Width: 18cm, height: 2cm
- Color: #E3F2FD (light blue)
- Label: "Awareness | 100,000 visitors" - black text
- Side: "100%" (right)

**Stage 2: Interest**
- Width: 15cm, height: 2cm
- Color: #90CAF9 (medium blue)
- Label: "Interest | 25,000 signups"
- Side: "25%" (right), "-75,000 (-75%)" (left, red)

**Stages 3-5:** Progressive narrowing

**Final Stage: Loyalty**
- Width: 6cm, height: 2cm
- Color: #003B5C (navy)
- Label: "Loyalty | 2,500 repeat customers" - white text
- Side: "2.5%" (cumulative conversion)

**Annotation:**
- Arrow pointing to Stage 2→3 drop: "Largest leak: Onboarding friction" - 10pt, #D32F2F
