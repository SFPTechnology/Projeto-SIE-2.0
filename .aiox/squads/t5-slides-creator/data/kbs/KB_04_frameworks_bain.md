# KB_04: Frameworks Bain - Results Delivery Focus

## Net Promoter Score (NPS)

### Origem e Conceito
**Desenvolvido por:** Fred Reichheld (Bain Partner, 2003)
**Publicado em:** Harvard Business Review - "The One Number You Need to Grow"
**Revolução:** Substituiu complexos surveys por 1 pergunta simples

### A Pergunta Ultimate

**Texto exato:**
"Em uma escala de 0 a 10, qual a probabilidade de você recomendar [nossa empresa/produto/serviço] para um amigo ou colega?"

**Scale:**
- 0 = Extremamente improvável
- 5 = Neutro
- 10 = Extremamente provável

### Classificação dos Respondentes

#### PROMOTERS (9-10)
**Características:**
- Leais, entusiastas, repeat buyers
- Fazem referrals ativamente
- Lifetime value 2-3× maior que Passives
- Toleram erros ocasionais
- Growth drivers

**Comportamento:**
- Buy more, mais frequente
- Share positive experiences (WOM marketing)
- Provide constructive feedback
- Menos price-sensitive

**Economic impact:**
- Retention rate: 90-95%
- Referral rate: 40-60% (trazem novos customers)
- Upsell success: 60-80%

#### PASSIVES (7-8)
**Características:**
- Satisfeitos mas não entusiasmados
- Vulneráveis a competitors
- Não fazem referrals proativamente
- Neutral/Silent

**Comportamento:**
- Buy ocasionalmente
- Switch se better offer appears
- Não complaint, mas não praise
- Price-sensitive

**Economic impact:**
- Retention: 70-80%
- Referral rate: 5-15% (apenas se asked)
- Upsell success: 30-40%

**Perigo:** Zona de indiferença → Can flip either way

#### DETRACTORS (0-6)
**Características:**
- Insatisfeitos, frustrated
- Prejudicam marca (negative WOM)
- High churn risk
- Absorb support resources

**Comportamento:**
- Complaint vocalmente (social media, reviews)
- Desencorajam prospects
- Exigem compensation/fixes
- Leave at first opportunity

**Economic impact:**
- Retention: 20-40%
- Negative WOM: 1 detractor = 10-15 negative interactions
- Cost to support: 3-5× higher than Promoters

### Cálculo do NPS

**Fórmula:**
```
NPS = % Promoters - % Detractors

(Passives não entram no cálculo diretamente)
```

**Exemplo:**
- Survey: 1,000 respondentes
- Score 9-10 (Promoters): 600 pessoas = 60%
- Score 7-8 (Passives): 250 pessoas = 25%
- Score 0-6 (Detractors): 150 pessoas = 15%

**NPS = 60% - 15% = +45**

### Interpretação e Benchmarks

**Range:** -100 (todos detractors) a +100 (todos promoters)

**Classification:**
- **Excelente:** 70-100 (World-class)
  - Exemplos: Apple, Tesla, Amazon Prime

- **Bom:** 50-70 (Strong)
  - Above average, healthy growth

- **Aceitável:** 30-50 (Average/Industry dependent)
  - Room for improvement

- **Pobre:** 0-30 (Problem)
  - Urgent action needed

- **Crítico:** Negative (Crisis)
  - Immediate intervention

**Benchmarks por indústria (US, 2024):**
- Software/SaaS: 40-50
- E-commerce: 45-55
- Retail: 30-40
- Banking: 25-35
- Telecom: 15-25 (typically lower)
- Airlines: 20-35
- Hotels: 35-50
- Healthcare: 30-40

**Top performers (70+):**
- USAA: 75-80
- Apple: 70-75
- Costco: 70-75
- Tesla: 90+ (outlier)

### Drivers Analysis

**Typical approach:**
1. Survey NPS
2. Ask follow-up: "Why did you give this score?"
3. Categorize themes
4. Quantify impact de cada driver

**Exemplo - SaaS Company:**

| Driver | % Mentioned | Promoter Correlation | Priority |
|--------|-------------|---------------------|----------|
| Customer support | 68% | +0.85 | 🔴 High |
| Ease of use | 54% | +0.72 | 🔴 High |
| Reliability | 48% | +0.65 | 🟡 Medium |
| Features | 42% | +0.45 | 🟡 Medium |
| Pricing | 38% | +0.32 | 🟢 Low |

**Insight:** Customer support é maior driver (68% mencionam, alta correlação)

### NPS Program Best Practices

**Survey design:**
- Timing: Post-purchase, post-support, quarterly (relationship NPS)
- Sample size: 300+ para confidence
- Response rate target: >25%
- Follow-up open-ended question ALWAYS

**Closing the loop:**
- Detractors: Contact within 24-48h
- Passives: Understand why not Promoters
- Promoters: Thank, ask for referral, case study

**Avoid gaming:**
- Don't incentivize specific scores
- Survey random samples (não apenas happy customers)
- Anonymous option
- External administration (third-party)

## Repeatable Models (Scalable Formulae)

### Conceito Core
**Insight Bain:** Empresas que crescem fast AND profitable têm uma "fórmula" que repetem.

**Componentes de um Repeatable Model:**

#### 1. FÓRMULA VENCEDORA (Differentiation)
**O que fazemos que é único e valuable?**

**Starbucks example:**
- "Third place" (não home, não work)
- Premium coffee
- Consistent experience globally

**Domino's example:**
- "30 minutes or free" (speed promise)
- Technology-first (online ordering desde early)
- Franchisee-friendly economics

**Netflix example:**
- Personalized recommendations
- Binge-worthy original content
- No ads, flat pricing

#### 2. CAPABILITIES CRÍTICAS (Como entregamos)
**3-6 capabilities que suportam fórmula**

**Starbucks:**
- Barista training (extensive, consistent)
- Store design (replicable, branded)
- Supply chain (fresh coffee globally)
- Real estate (high-traffic locations)

**Amazon:**
- Logistics network (speed, cost)
- Technology platform (scale, reliability)
- Customer obsession (culture)
- Data analytics (personalization)

#### 3. RECURSOS NECESSÁRIOS (Assets, capital)
**Physical, financial, human resources**

**McDonald's:**
- Real estate (own land, lease restaurants)
- Supply chain partnerships (potato farms, beef)
- Training infrastructure (Hamburger University)
- Capital: $2-3M per new restaurant

#### 4. PROCESSO REPETÍVEL (Como replicamos)
**Step-by-step playbook**

**Exemplo - Retail expansion:**
- **Month 1-2:** Market analysis (demographics, traffic, competitors)
- **Month 3-4:** Site selection (criteria: 50K people within 5 miles, 20K cars/day)
- **Month 5-6:** Store design (standard 2,000 sqft layout)
- **Month 7-9:** Build-out (contractors, equipment, fixtures)
- **Month 10:** Hiring (15 FTE, 2 weeks training)
- **Month 11:** Soft launch (friends & family)
- **Month 12:** Grand opening
- **Result:** $2M annual revenue, 18-month payback

### Metrics de Repeatability

**Unit Economics:**
- Revenue per unit: Consistente? ($1.8-2.2M = 20% variance OK)
- Cost per unit: Predictable? ($1.2M ± 10%)
- Payback: <24 meses ideal
- ROI: >30% target

**Success Rate:**
- New units profitable: >80% após 18 meses
- Failure rate: <10%
- Ramp-up time: Diminuindo? (12 → 9 → 6 meses)

**Scalability:**
- Manager bench: 3× pipeline vs current needs
- Supply chain: Pode 2× sem breaking?
- Capital: Funding secured para next 50 units?

## Founder's Mentality

### Conceito
**Autores:** Chris Zook & James Allen (Bain partners, livro 2016)

**Insight:** Empresas perdem "soul" ao crescer → Burocracy, politics, silos.

### Os 3 Elementos

#### 1. INSURGENCY (Missão de Mudar o Mundo)
**Características:**
- Underdog mentality (nós vs establishment)
- Missão inspiradora (não apenas $$$)
- Senso de urgência
- Todos sabem o "why"

**Lost quando:**
- "We're #1" → Complacency
- Mission virou corporate speak
- Strategy = protect turf (não disrupt)

**Restaurar:**
- Redefine enemy (complacency, new disruptor)
- Refresh mission statement
- CEO communica pessoalmente
- Town halls, storytelling

**Exemplo - Microsoft (Satya Nadella):**
- Before: "We're entrenched, protect Windows"
- After: "Empower every person and organization" → Cloud-first, mobile-first

#### 2. FRONTLINE OBSESSION (Proximidade com Batalha)
**Características:**
- Leaders conhecem frontline intimamente
- Decisões based on frontline reality (não HQ theory)
- Fast feedback loops
- Frontline tem voice

**Lost quando:**
- Layers entre CEO e customer (6+ levels)
- HQ office vs field disconnect
- Policies sem frontline input
- Metrics matter more than reality

**Restaurar:**
- CEO spends time com customers/frontline
- Skip-level meetings regulares
- Frontline innovation programs
- Rotate execs to field roles

**Exemplo - Walmart (Doug McMillon):**
- Visita 2-3 lojas por semana pessoalmente
- "I learn more in store aisles than conference rooms"
- Store manager direct line to CEO

#### 3. OWNER MINDSET (Pensar como Dono)
**Características:**
- Accountability total (não "não é meu job")
- Long-term thinking (não quarterly games)
- Frugality (spend como se seu $)
- Performance = rewards

**Lost quando:**
- "That's not my department"
- Short-term incentives dominant
- Bureaucracy slows decisions
- No consequence para mediocrity

**Restaurar:**
- P&L por unit/team (mini-CEOs)
- Equity ownership (RSUs, ESOP)
- Performance-based comp (não tenure)
- Clear ownership matrix

**Exemplo - Amazon:**
- Two-pizza teams (ownership)
- "Day 1" mentality (sempre startup)
- Frugality principle (door desks, não mahogany)

### Metrics de Founder's Mentality

**Insurgency:**
- Employee survey: "I believe in company mission" >80%
- Attrition de top performers: <10%
- Innovation index: # de new ideas per employee

**Frontline Obsession:**
- Exec time com customers: >30% do tempo
- Frontline NPS (employee): >50
- Decision speed: <7 days para 80% das decisões

**Owner Mindset:**
- Equity participation: >70% de employees
- ROIC per unit: >20%
- Revenue per employee: Top quartile

## Results Delivery Framework

### Obsessão Bain: ROI Tangível

**Princípio:** Consulting deve gerar 5-10× o fee paid.

**Approach:**
1. Quantify baseline (atual state)
2. Set target ambicioso MAS atingível
3. Initiatives com clear owners + timelines
4. Weekly KPI tracking
5. Course correct fast

### Typical Engagement Structure

**Phase 1: Diagnostic (4-6 weeks)**
- Current state assessment
- Opportunity sizing
- Quick wins identification
- Business case

**Phase 2: Design (6-8 weeks)**
- Detailed design de initiatives
- Pilot testing
- Change management planning
- Refine financials

**Phase 3: Implementation (12-18 months)**
- Full rollout
- Weekly steering
- KPI dashboards
- Capture realized value

## Bain Private Equity Toolkit

### Buy-and-Build Strategy

**Concept:** Acquire platform + bolt-ons para create value via consolidation + synergies.

**Playbook:**
1. **Platform acquisition** (strong management, $50-200M EBITDA)
2. **Operational improvements** (year 1-2, +$20-50M EBITDA)
3. **Add-on acquisitions** (3-7 bolt-ons, $5-20M EBITDA cada)
4. **Integration + synergies** ($10-30M from cost/revenue synergies)
5. **Exit** (strategic sale ou IPO, year 4-6)

**Value creation sources:**
- Operational (40-50%): Margin expansion, efficiency
- Synergies (20-30%): Cost consolidation, cross-sell
- Multiple expansion (20-30%): Larger business = higher multiple
- Leverage (5-10%): Debt paydown

### 100-Day Plan (Post-Acquisition)

**Day 1-30: Stabilize**
- Retain key talent (retention bonuses)
- Communicate to employees, customers
- Quick assessment (где problems/opportunities)
- No major changes yet

**Day 31-60: Plan**
- Detailed diagnostic (operational, financial)
- Initiative identification (10-15 initiatives)
- Owner assignment
- Resource planning

**Day 61-100: Execute**
- Launch quick wins (3-5 initiatives)
- Set up governance (weekly steering, KPIs)
- Realize first $5-10M value
- Build momentum

**Target:** 20-30% of total value creation no primeiro ano

### Exit Readiness (Day 1)

**Concept:** Prepare for exit desde aquisição, não last minute.

**Workstreams:**
1. **Story:** Growth narrative, market positioning
2. **Financials:** Clean GAAP, adjusted EBITDA bridge
3. **Operations:** Best-in-class metrics
4. **Management:** Strong team (promotable CFO, COO)
5. **Pipeline:** Organic + M&A opportunities visible

**Exit múltiplos (EBITDA):**
- Entry: 8-10×
- Exit (if well executed): 12-15×
- Multiple expansion = 20-30% do IRR
