# KB_12: Retail & Consumer - Omnichannel, E-commerce, CPG

## Retail KPIs Fundamentais

### Same-Store Sales (SSS) / Comparable Store Sales

**Definição:** Vendas de lojas abertas há 12+ meses (exclui novos stores)

**Cálculo:**
```
SSS Growth % = [(Sales_current - Sales_prior) / Sales_prior] × 100
```

**Benchmarks:**
- Excelente: >5% growth
- Saudável: 2-5%
- Flat: -2% to +2%
- Declining: <-2%

**Drivers breakdown:**
- Traffic (footfall): -5% to +10% típico
- Conversion rate: 20-40% (varies by category)
- Basket size: $30-150 (varies)
- Items per transaction: 2-8

**Slide tipo:**
**Título:** "SSS de +6.2% impulsionado por traffic (+2.5pp) e basket size (+3.7pp), superando peers em 4pp"

### Sales Per Square Foot

**Cálculo:**
```
Sales/SF = Annual Sales / Total Selling Square Footage
```

**Benchmarks por formato:**
- Luxury retail: $1,000-3,000/SF
- Department stores: $200-400/SF
- Grocery: $400-800/SF
- Apple stores: $5,000-6,000/SF (best-in-class)
- Mall specialty: $300-600/SF

**Trends:** E-commerce pressure → Need $500+/SF para viabilidade

### Inventory Turnover

**Cálculo:**
```
Inventory Turnover = COGS / Average Inventory
```

**Benchmarks:**
- Grocery/perishables: 15-25× por ano
- Apparel: 4-6×
- Furniture: 2-4×
- Electronics: 6-10×

**Days Inventory Outstanding (DIO):**
```
DIO = 365 / Inventory Turnover
```

**Fast fashion (Zara, H&M):** 4-6 weeks
**Traditional apparel:** 90-120 dias
**Luxury:** 180+ dias (OK devido a margin)

**Slide tipo:**
**Título:** "Inventory turnover de 8× (45 dias) vs 6× peers libera $15M working capital e reduz markdown risk"

### Gross Margin Return on Investment (GMROI)

**Fórmula:**
```
GMROI = Gross Margin $ / Average Inventory Cost
```

**Interpretação:**
- \>3.0: Excelente (cada $1 em inventory gera $3 margin)
- 2.0-3.0: Saudável
- 1.5-2.0: Aceitável
- <1.5: Problema (não recovering inventory cost)

**Exemplo:**
- Gross margin: $500K
- Avg inventory: $200K
- GMROI = $500K / $200K = 2.5

### Customer Lifetime Value (Retail)

**Cálculo:**
```
LTV = (Avg Purchase Value × Purchase Frequency × Gross Margin %) / Churn Rate
```

**Exemplo:**
- Avg transaction: $75
- Frequency: 6× por ano
- Gross margin: 45%
- Churn: 25% anual
- LTV = ($75 × 6 × 45%) / 25% = $810

**LTV by segment:**
- Grocery (frequent, low margin): $200-500
- Apparel (moderate): $300-1,000
- Luxury (high margin, loyal): $2,000-10,000

## E-commerce Metrics

### Conversion Rate

**Fórmula:**
```
Conversion Rate = Orders / Sessions × 100%
```

**Benchmarks:**
- Desktop: 3-5%
- Mobile: 1-3% (lower, but growing)
- Tablet: 4-6%
- Overall blended: 2-4%

**Top performers:** 5-10%

**Micro-conversions:**
- Add-to-cart: 8-15%
- Cart-to-checkout: 40-60%
- Checkout-to-order: 60-80%

### Average Order Value (AOV)

**Cálculo:**
```
AOV = Total Revenue / Number of Orders
```

**Tactics para aumentar:**
- Upsell/cross-sell: +15-25% AOV
- Free shipping threshold: +10-20%
- Bundling: +20-35%
- Loyalty rewards: +8-15%

**Benchmarks:**
- Apparel: $50-100
- Beauty: $40-80
- Electronics: $200-500
- Furniture: $500-1,500

### Cart Abandonment Rate

**Cálculo:**
```
Abandonment Rate = (Carts Created - Orders) / Carts Created × 100%
```

**Benchmark:** 60-80% (sim, é alto!)

**Razões principais:**
1. Unexpected shipping costs: 55%
2. Creating account required: 35%
3. Complicated checkout: 28%
4. Lack of trust: 18%
5. Price comparison: 40%

**Recovery tactics:**
- Abandoned cart emails: 8-15% conversion
- Retargeting ads: 2-5%
- SMS reminders: 10-20% (if opted in)

**Slide tipo:**
**Título:** "Abandoned cart recovery program (emails + retargeting) recupera $2.5M anual (12% de carts), ROI de 8:1"

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) - Retail

**Cálculo:**
```
CAC = (Marketing + Sales Expenses) / New Customers Acquired
```

**Benchmarks por canal:**
- Organic search: $10-30
- Paid search: $30-80
- Social media ads: $20-60
- Influencer: $40-120
- Email (existing list): $5-15
- Referral: $15-40

**Payback period:** 6-18 meses típico

**LTV:CAC ratio ideal:** >3:1

## Omnichannel Performance

### BOPIS / Click & Collect

**BOPIS:** Buy Online, Pick up In Store

**Metrics:**
- BOPIS penetration: 10-30% de online sales
- In-store attachment rate: 20-40% (compram algo extra)
- Fulfillment time: <2h target
- No-shows: <5%

**Economics:**
- Shipping cost avoided: $5-15 per order
- Incremental margin (attachment): $8-25
- Labor cost (pick & pack): $3-8

**Net benefit:** $5-20 por BOPIS order

### Ship-from-Store

**Benefits:**
- Faster delivery (1-2 dias vs 3-5)
- Lower shipping cost (closer to customer)
- Reduce excess inventory in stores

**Challenges:**
- Store labor: $5-12 per order
- Inventory accuracy: Requires 99%+
- Systems integration

**Penetration:** 15-40% de online orders nas top retailers

### Unified Commerce

**Definition:** Single view of inventory, customer, orders

**Metrics:**
- Inventory accuracy: >98% required
- Order visibility: Real-time
- Customer data sync: <5min delay

**ROI:**
- Reduced out-of-stocks: +2-5% sales
- Lower markdowns: +1-3% margin
- Better personalization: +10-20% conversion

## Consumer Packaged Goods (CPG) Specifics

### Nielsen/IRI Syndicated Data

**Key metrics:**
- **TDP (Total Distribution Points):** % de lojas que carregam SKU × % ACV (All Commodity Volume)
  - Example: 80% das lojas × 90% ACV = 72 TDP
  - Target new launch: 60+ TDP em 6 meses

- **Velocity:** Units per point of distribution
  - Fast-moving: >5 units/week/store
  - Slow: <2 units/week/store

- **Market Share:**
  - Dollar share vs Unit share (revela price positioning)
  - Gaining share: +0.5pp YoY = strong

### Promotional Effectiveness

**Lift:**
```
Lift % = (Sales during promo - Baseline) / Baseline × 100%
```

**Benchmarks:**
- Price discount (20% off): 50-150% lift
- Display only: 30-80%
- Feature (circular ad): 100-300%
- Feature + Display: 200-500%

**ROI de promo:**
```
ROI = (Incremental Margin - Promo Cost) / Promo Cost
```

**Threshold:** >25% ROI para aprovar

**Slide tipo:**
**Título:** "Promoção de feature+display gera 280% lift e ROI de 45%, vs desconto puro (120% lift, 15% ROI)"

### Trade Spend Efficiency

**Trade spend:** 15-25% de gross sales (CPG average)

**Breakdown:**
- Slotting fees: 10-20% de trade
- Off-invoice discounts: 40-50%
- Scan-based allowances: 15-25%
- Co-op advertising: 10-15%
- Others: 10-20%

**Redemption rate:** 60-80% típico (20-40% fica como "slush")

**Optimization:**
- Shift to performance-based (scan vs off-invoice)
- Reduce breadth, increase depth
- Focus on high-velocity retailers

## Loyalty & Personalization

### Loyalty Program Metrics

**Enrollment rate:** 30-60% of transactions
**Active rate:** 40-70% of enrolled (transacted in last 90 dias)

**Member value:**
- Frequency: 2-3× vs non-members
- AOV: +15-30%
- Margin: +5-10% (higher full-price purchases)

**Program cost:** 1-3% of sales

**Net benefit:** +10-25% profit from members

### RFM Segmentation

**Recency, Frequency, Monetary:**
- **Champions:** High on all 3
- **Loyal:** High F & M, moderate R
- **At-risk:** High F & M, low R (haven't purchased recently)
- **Lost:** Low on all

**Slide tipo:**
**Título:** "Champions (12% of base) drive 45% of revenue; at-risk (18%) retention program recupera $8M annually"

### Personalization Impact

**Email:**
- Segmented vs batch: +20-50% open rates
- Personalized subject: +10-30% opens
- Product recommendations: +15-40% CTR

**Site:**
- Personalized homepage: +5-15% conversion
- Dynamic pricing: +2-8% revenue (se done well)
- Recommendations: 10-30% of revenue (Amazon: ~35%)

## Pricing & Promotions

### Price Elasticity

**Cálculo:**
```
Elasticity = % Change in Quantity / % Change in Price
```

**Ranges típicos:**
- Luxury: -0.2 to -0.5 (inelastic)
- Mainstream apparel: -1.0 to -1.5 (elastic)
- Commodities: -1.5 to -3.0 (highly elastic)

**Optimal price:**
- Maximize revenue: Elasticity = -1
- Maximize margin: Depends on cost structure

### Markdown Optimization

**Markdown %:**
- Apparel: 20-35% of sales
- Luxury: 10-20%
- Off-price (TJX): 60-70% (but bought cheaper)

**Timing:**
- Early markdown: Larger pool, smaller discount (30% off)
- Late markdown: Smaller pool, larger discount (50-70% off)

**AI-driven:** Dynamic markdowns based on inventory age, velocity, weather, etc.
- Benefit: +2-5% margin vs static rules

## Terminologia Especializada

**SKU:** Stock Keeping Unit
**UPC:** Universal Product Code
**ACV:** All Commodity Volume (total $ sales in category)
**AUR:** Average Unit Retail (price per item)
**IMU:** Initial Markup (retail - cost) / retail
**Maintained margin:** After markdowns
**Shrink:** Inventory loss (theft, damage) - target <2%
**Fill rate:** % of orders shipped complete
**On-time delivery:** >95% target
**Lead time:** Weeks from order to receipt
**MOQ:** Minimum Order Quantity
**DTC:** Direct-to-Consumer
**D2C:** Direct to Consumer (same as DTC)
**Dropship:** Vendor ships directly to customer
**3PL:** Third-Party Logistics
**Omnichannel:** Integrated across channels
**Clienteling:** Personalized service by associates
**Showrooming:** Check in store, buy online
**Webrooming:** Research online, buy in store
**EDLP:** Every Day Low Price (Walmart strategy)
**HiLo:** High-Low pricing (frequent promos)

- Eixo X: Sales growth %

- Quadrants: Stars (high/high), Dogs (low/low), etc.

- Homepage → Category → Product → Cart → Checkout → Order
