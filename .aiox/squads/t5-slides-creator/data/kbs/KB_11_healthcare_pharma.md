# KB_11: Healthcare & Pharmaceutical - Clinical, Operations, R&D

## Patient Outcome Metrics

### Quality of Care

**HEDIS (Healthcare Effectiveness Data and Information Set):**
- 90+ medidas padronizadas
- Usado por 90% dos health plans US
- Categorias: Prevention, chronic disease, behavioral health

**Principais medidas:**
- HbA1c control (<8% para diabéticos): Target >70% dos pacientes
- Blood pressure control (<140/90): Target >65%
- Cholesterol management: Target >60%
- Cancer screening rates: Target >75%
- Immunization rates: Target >90%

**Readmission Rate:**
- Definição: Readmissão em 30 dias pós-alta
- Benchmark: <15% (all-cause)
- Penalidades CMS se >benchmark

**Hospital-Acquired Conditions (HACs):**
- CLABSI: Central Line-Associated Bloodstream Infections
- CAUTI: Catheter-Associated UTIs
- SSI: Surgical Site Infections
- Target: Zero (cada caso = quality failure)

**Patient Safety Indicators (PSIs):**
- Pressure ulcers: <2%
- Falls with injury: <3 per 1,000 patient-days
- Medication errors: <5 per 1,000 doses

### Patient Satisfaction

**HCAHPS (Hospital Consumer Assessment):**
- 32 perguntas padronizadas
- Scale: 0-10 ou Never/Sometimes/Usually/Always
- Public reporting obrigatório (CMS)

**Top Box % (% "Always" ou "9-10"):**
- Excelente: >80%
- Média: 60-70%
- Pobre: <50%

**Net Promoter Score (Healthcare):**
- Benchmark hospitais: 30-50
- Top performers: >60
- Clinics/ambulatório: 40-60

**Título:** "HCAHPS top box de 72% (+8pp vs ano anterior) impulsionado por nurse communication e discharge planning"

## Operational Efficiency

### Capacity & Utilization

**Bed Occupancy Rate:**
- Fórmula: (Patient-days / Bed-days available) × 100
- Optimal: 85-90%
- >95%: Overcrowding, quality issues
- <70%: Ineficiência financeira

**OR (Operating Room) Utilization:**
- Target: 70-80% das horas disponíveis
- Prime time (7am-5pm): >85%
- Turnover time: <30min entre cirurgias

**Length of Stay (LOS):**
- Varia por DRG (Diagnosis-Related Group)
- Exemplo: Hip replacement: 2-3 dias
- Benchmark: Geometric mean do CMS
- Cada dia extra: $2-5K de custo

**Emergency Department Metrics:**
- Door-to-doctor time: <30min (target)
- Left Without Being Seen (LWBS): <2%
- Boarding time (ED to inpatient): <4h
- Total ED length of stay: <4h (median)

### Labor Productivity

**Worked Hours Per Patient Day (HPPD):**
- Total nursing hours / Patient-days
- Med-surg unit: 8-10 HPPD
- ICU: 18-24 HPPD
- Step-down: 12-16 HPPD

**FTE per Occupied Bed:**
- Benchmark: 4.5-5.5 FTEs (full-time equivalents)
- Teaching hospitals: 5.5-7.0
- Community hospitals: 4.0-5.0

**Labor Cost as % of Revenue:**
- Target: 50-55%
- >60%: Problema estrutural
- <45%: Possível understaffing

### Financial Performance

**Operating Margin:**
- Saudável: 3-5%
- Top quartile: >6%
- Breakeven: 0-2%
- Distressed: <0%

**Days in A/R (Accounts Receivable):**
- Best practice: <40 dias
- Acceptable: 40-50 dias
- Problema: >60 dias

**Payor Mix Impact:**
- Medicare: Reimbursa 87-90% de custo
- Medicaid: 85-88%
- Commercial: 120-150%
- Uninsured/Charity: 0-20%

## Pharmaceutical R&D

### Drug Development Pipeline

**Fases Clínicas:**

**Phase I (Safety):**
- Participants: 20-100 healthy volunteers
- Duration: 6-12 meses
- Success rate: ~70% → Phase II
- Cost: $10-20M

**Phase II (Efficacy & Dosing):**
- Participants: 100-500 patients
- Duration: 1-2 anos
- Success rate: ~33% → Phase III
- Cost: $30-60M

**Phase III (Confirmatory):**
- Participants: 1,000-5,000 patients
- Duration: 2-4 anos
- Success rate: ~60% → FDA approval
- Cost: $100-300M

**Phase IV (Post-marketing):**
- Post-approval surveillance
- Long-term safety, additional indications

**Overall probability (Phase I → Approval):** ~10-15%

**Total time:** 10-15 anos (discovery → market)

**Total cost:** $1-2.5B (including failures)

### Pipeline Value

**Net Present Value (NPV) de cada asset:**

**Fórmula:**
```
NPV = Σ [(Revenue × Probability of Success) - Costs] / (1 + Discount Rate)^t
```

**Componentes:**
- Peak sales estimate: $500M - $5B (blockbuster: >$1B)
- PoS (Probability of Success): 10-60% dependendo de fase
- Time to peak: 5-10 anos pós-launch
- Patent cliff: Generic entry ano 12-15
- Discount rate: 8-12%

**Slide tipo:**
**Título:** "Pipeline NPV de $8B com 3 assets em Phase III (80% do valor) e Phase I balance de risco/upside"

### R&D Productivity

**R&D as % of Revenue:**
- Big pharma: 15-20%
- Biotech: 30-80% (pre-revenue)
- Generics: 5-8%

**Cost per Approved Drug:**
- Aumentando ~9% ao ano
- 2024: ~$2.5B (fully-loaded)
- Breakdown: 65% clinical, 25% discovery, 10% regulatory

**Cycle Time Compression:**
- Traditional: 12-15 anos
- Accelerated (rare disease): 8-10 anos
- Breakthrough designation: -2 anos no processo

## Health Economics & Outcomes Research (HEOR)

### Cost-Effectiveness Analysis

**QALY (Quality-Adjusted Life Year):**
- 1 QALY = 1 ano em perfeita saúde
- Weights: 0 (dead) to 1 (perfect health)

**ICER (Incremental Cost-Effectiveness Ratio):**
```
ICER = (Cost_new - Cost_standard) / (QALY_new - QALY_standard)
```

**Willingness-to-Pay thresholds:**
- UK (NICE): £20-30K per QALY
- US (informal): $50-150K per QALY
- Rare diseases: Até $500K aceitável

**Exemplo:**
- Drug A: $100K/ano, 2.5 QALYs
- Standard care: $30K/ano, 1.8 QALYs
- ICER = ($100K - $30K) / (2.5 - 1.8) = $100K per QALY

**Slide tipo:**
**Título:** "ICER de $85K/QALY está abaixo de threshold de $100K, supporting reimbursement approval em 80% dos payors"

### Budget Impact Model

**Componentes:**
- Eligible population size
- Market share trajectory (anos 1-5)
- Cost per patient per year
- Offset savings (reduced hospitalizations, etc.)

**Net budget impact = Total drug cost - Offsets**

**Payors focam em:** Impacto nos primeiros 3 anos

## Value-Based Care Models

### ACO (Accountable Care Organization)

**Conceito:** Provider network assumes risk por quality + cost

**Shared Savings:**
- Benchmark: Historical spending
- Target: Reduzir 2-5%
- Split: 50-60% para providers

**Quality gates:** Mínimo 85% em HEDIS measures para qualify

### Bundled Payments

**Episódios típicos:**
- Hip/knee replacement: $20-30K bundle
- CABG: $30-50K
- Maternity: $8-15K

**Inclui:** Hospital + physician + post-acute (90 dias)

**Risk:** Provider assume se custo > bundle price

### Capitation

**Per-Member-Per-Month (PMPM):**
- Primary care: $30-60 PMPM
- Specialty care: $50-150 PMPM
- Full risk: $400-800 PMPM (all services)

**Medical Loss Ratio (MLR):**
```
MLR = Medical Costs / Premium Revenue
```
- ACA minimum: 80% (individual), 85% (large group)
- Insurer margin = 100% - MLR - Admin costs

## Regulatory & Compliance

### FDA Approval Pathways

**Standard NDA/BLA:** 10 meses review
**Priority Review:** 6 meses (serious conditions)
**Accelerated Approval:** Baseado em surrogate endpoints
**Breakthrough Therapy:** Expedited development + review

**Orphan Drug Designation:**
- <200K patients nos US
- Benefits: 7 anos exclusivity, tax credits, fee waivers

### HIPAA Compliance

**Protected Health Information (PHI):**
- 18 identifiers que devem ser removed/encrypted
- Breach penalties: $100-50,000 per violation
- Caps: $1.5M per year

**Security Rule:**
- Administrative safeguards (access controls, training)
- Physical safeguards (facility access, workstation security)
- Technical safeguards (encryption, audit logs)

### CMS Quality Programs

**Hospital Readmissions Reduction Program:**
- Penalidade: Até 3% de Medicare payments
- Conditions: AMI, heart failure, pneumonia, COPD, hip/knee, CABG

**Hospital Value-Based Purchasing:**
- 2% of payments at risk
- Domains: Clinical outcomes, patient experience, safety, efficiency

## Terminologia Especializada

**DRG:** Diagnosis-Related Group (payment grouping)
**APR-DRG:** All Patient Refined DRG (com severity)
**ICD-10:** International Classification of Diseases, 10th revision
**CPT:** Current Procedural Terminology (coding)
**EMR/EHR:** Electronic Medical/Health Record
**HL7:** Health Level 7 (interoperability standard)
**FHIR:** Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources
**HIE:** Health Information Exchange
**RCM:** Revenue Cycle Management
**Prior authorization:** Payor approval antes de tratamento
**Step therapy:** Try cheaper drug first antes de expensive
**Formulary:** Lista de drugs covered por payor
**Specialty pharmacy:** High-cost drugs ($500+/mês)
**Biosimilar:** "Generic" de biologic drug
**Indication:** Approved use of drug
**Off-label:** Use fora de indication aprovada
**Endpoints:** Outcomes medidos em trials
**Surrogate endpoint:** Marker instead of true outcome (ex: tumor shrinkage vs survival)
**Pharmacovigilance:** Post-market safety monitoring
**Adverse event:** Problema de saúde durante trial
**Serious AE:** Death, hospitalization, disability
**Comparator:** Drug usado como comparison em trial
**Placebo:** Inactive treatment para control arm
**Blinding:** Double-blind = patient + doctor não sabem treatment
**Randomization:** Allocation aleatória para treatment arms
**Intention-to-treat:** Analyze all randomized patients (even if stopped)
**Per-protocol:** Analyze only compliant patients
