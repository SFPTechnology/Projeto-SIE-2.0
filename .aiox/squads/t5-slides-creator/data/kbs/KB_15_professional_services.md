# KB_15: Professional Services - Consulting, Legal, Accounting, Advisory

## Core Productivity Metrics

### Utilization Rate

**Definição:** Billable hours / Total available hours

**Cálculo:**
```
Utilization Rate = Billable Hours / (Total Hours - PTO - Holidays) × 100%
```

**Benchmarks por role:**

**Junior (Analyst/Associate):**
- Target: 80-90%
- Top performers: >90%
- Threshold mínimo: 70%

**Mid-level (Consultant/Manager):**
- Target: 70-80%
- Aceitável: 65-75%
- Includes: Business development, training

**Senior (Partner/Principal):**
- Target: 50-65%
- Mais tempo em BD, firm management
- Billable hours: 1,000-1,300/ano

**Firm-wide blended:**
- Consulting: 60-75%
- Legal: 65-80%
- Accounting (busy season): 80-95%

**Available hours calculation:**
- Total year: 2,080 hours (52 weeks × 40h)
- Less PTO: -160h (4 weeks)
- Less holidays: -80h (10 dias)
- Less training: -80h (2 weeks)
- Available: 1,760 hours
- Target billable (75%): 1,320 hours

**Slide tipo:**
**Título:** "Utilization de 73% (vs 68% ano anterior) impulsionada por pipeline improvement e resource allocation, generating $8M incremental revenue"

### Realization Rate

**Definição:** Actual revenue / Standard billing rate

**Cálculo:**
```
Realization Rate = Revenue Collected / (Hours × Standard Rate) × 100%
```

**Drivers de realization <100%:**
- Write-downs (quality issues, scope creep)
- Write-offs (uncollectible)
- Discounts (negotiated rates)
- Fixed-fee projects (overruns)

**Benchmarks:**
- Excellent: >95%
- Good: 90-95%
- Acceptable: 85-90%
- Problem: <85%

**By service type:**
- Hourly billing: 90-100%
- Fixed-fee: 70-90% (depends on scoping accuracy)
- Retainer: 85-95%
- Contingent: 100% se ganhar, 0% se perder

**Improvement levers:**
- Scoping accuracy: +3-8%
- Client communication: +2-5%
- Quality delivery: +5-10%
- Collection process: +1-3%

### Effective Rate

**Definição:** Blended rate após discounts + write-downs

**Cálculo:**
```
Effective Rate = Total Revenue / Total Hours Worked
```

**vs Standard Rate:**
```
Effective Rate = Standard Rate × Realization Rate
```

**Exemplo:**
- Standard rate: $300/hour
- Realization: 92%
- Effective rate: $300 × 92% = $276/hour

**Tracking:** Por client, por project type, por partner

**Slide tipo:**
**Título:** "Effective rate de $285/h (+$22 vs 2023) driven por mix shift para strategy work e improved realization (90% → 93%)"

### Revenue Per Employee (RPE)

**Cálculo:**
```
RPE = Total Revenue / Total FTEs
```

**Benchmarks:**

**Big 4 Consulting:**
- $180-250K per FTE

**Strategy consulting (MBB):**
- $400-600K per FTE

**Legal (AmLaw 100):**
- $500-900K per FTE (equity partners muito higher)

**Accounting:**
- $150-250K per FTE

**Boutique advisory:**
- $200-400K (varies widely)

**Drivers:**
- Leverage (ratio junior:senior)
- Billing rates
- Utilization
- Realization

## Leverage Model

### Pyramid Structure

**Typical consulting firm:**

**Partners/Principals:** 10-15%
- Bill at: $500-1,000+/hour
- Utilization: 50-65%
- Focus: BD, relationships, QA

**Managers/Senior Consultants:** 20-30%
- Bill at: $300-500/hour
- Utilization: 70-80%
- Focus: Project management, analysis

**Consultants/Associates:** 30-40%
- Bill at: $200-350/hour
- Utilization: 75-85%
- Focus: Analysis, deliverables

**Analysts:** 20-30%
- Bill at: $100-200/hour
- Utilization: 80-90%
- Focus: Research, data, models

**Leverage ratio:**
```
Leverage = Total Professionals / Partners
```

**Benchmarks:**
- Strategy (MBB): 4-6:1
- Big 4: 10-15:1
- Legal: 3-5:1

**Higher leverage → Higher profitability (se managed bem)**

### Blended Rate

**Cálculo weighted por hours:**
```
Blended Rate = Σ(Hours_role × Rate_role) / Total Hours
```

**Exemplo project team:**
- Partner: 100h × $800/h = $80K
- Manager: 300h × $400/h = $120K
- Consultant: 500h × $250/h = $125K
- Analyst: 600h × $150/h = $90K
- Total: 1,500h, $415K
- Blended: $415K / 1,500h = $277/hour

**Strategic pricing:**
- Higher blended = More senior team (quality, speed)
- Lower blended = More junior (cost-effective, training)

**Slide tipo:**
**Título:** "Blended rate de $310/h (+8% YoY) via mix shift para senior resources mantém 45% gross margin target"

## Client Metrics

### Client Concentration

**Cálculo:**
```
Concentration = Revenue from Top N Clients / Total Revenue
```

**Benchmarks:**
- Top 1 client: <15% (risk)
- Top 5 clients: <40%
- Top 10 clients: <60%

**Risk thresholds:**
- Any client >25%: High risk
- Any client >50%: Extreme risk

**Diversification value:**
- Reduces revenue volatility
- Negotiating leverage
- Sector expertise

### Wallet Share

**Definição:** Your revenue / Client's total spend in category

**Benchmarks:**
- Primary advisor: >50%
- Significant player: 20-50%
- Niche/project-based: <20%

**Growth strategies:**
- Cross-sell other services
- Expand to other BUs/geographies
- Increase frequency (retainers)

### Client Retention

**Cálculo:**
```
Retention Rate = Clients at End / Clients at Start × 100%
```

**Benchmarks:**
- Excellent: >90%
- Good: 80-90%
- Concerning: <80%

**Revenue retention (more important):**
```
Revenue Retention = This Year Revenue (same clients) / Last Year Revenue × 100%
```

**Can be >100% se upsell/expansion**

**Churn reasons:**
- Project completion (natural): 40-50%
- Budget constraints: 20-30%
- Relationship issues: 10-20%
- Quality concerns: 5-15%

## Financial Performance

### Gross Margin

**Cálculo:**
```
Gross Margin = (Revenue - Direct Labor) / Revenue × 100%
```

**Direct Labor:**
- Salaries + benefits de billable staff
- Excludes: Admin, marketing, facilities

**Benchmarks:**
- Strategy consulting: 50-65%
- IT consulting: 35-50%
- Legal: 50-70%
- Accounting: 40-55%

**Drivers:**
- Billing rates
- Utilization
- Leverage
- Salary costs

### EBITDA Margin

**Cálculo:**
```
EBITDA Margin = EBITDA / Revenue × 100%
```

**Benchmarks:**
- Strategy (MBB): 25-35%
- Big 4 consulting: 15-25%
- Legal (AmLaw 100): 25-40%
- Boutique advisory: 20-35%

**Profit per partner (PPP):**
- MBB: $2-5M+
- Big 4: $500K-1.5M
- Legal (AmLaw 50): $2-8M
- Boutique: $300K-2M

### Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)

**Cálculo:**
```
DSO = (Accounts Receivable / Revenue) × 365
```

**Benchmarks:**
- Excellent: <45 days
- Good: 45-60 days
- Acceptable: 60-90 days
- Problem: >90 days

**Collection strategies:**
- Net 30 terms
- Progress billing (monthly)
- Retainers (advance payment)
- Auto-pay setup

**Impact:**
- Each 10 days DSO = ~3% of revenue em working capital

**Slide tipo:**
**Título:** "DSO reduction de 72 para 58 dias libera $4.5M working capital via automated invoicing e payment terms enforcement"

## Business Development

### Pipeline Metrics

**Pipeline value:**
```
Pipeline = Σ(Opportunity Value × Probability of Win)
```

**Stage probabilities:**
- Lead/Inquiry: 10-20%
- Qualified: 25-35%
- Proposal submitted: 40-60%
- Finalist: 60-80%
- Verbal commitment: 85-95%

**Coverage ratio:**
```
Coverage = Weighted Pipeline / Revenue Target
```

**Healthy coverage:** 3-5× (depends on win rate, sales cycle)

### Win Rate

**Cálculo:**
```
Win Rate = Opportunities Won / Total Opportunities × 100%
```

**By stage:**
- All opportunities: 15-30%
- Qualified opps: 25-40%
- Proposals: 30-50%
- Finalist/shortlist: 40-70%

**By source:**
- Referrals: 40-60%
- Existing clients: 50-70%
- RFPs (cold): 10-25%
- Inbound: 30-50%

**Improvement levers:**
- Better qualification: +5-15%
- Stronger proposals: +3-10%
- Relationships: +10-20%

### Sales Cycle Length

**Benchmarks:**

**Strategy consulting:**
- Small project (<$100K): 1-3 meses
- Large engagement ($500K+): 3-9 meses
- Retainer: 2-6 meses

**Legal:**
- Transactional: 1-3 meses
- Litigation: 1-6 meses (longer)
- General counsel: 3-12 meses

**Accounting:**
- Audit: 6-12 meses (annual cycle)
- Tax: 1-3 meses
- Advisory: 2-6 meses

**Acceleration tactics:**
- Warm introductions: -30-50% cycle
- Free diagnostics: -20-40%
- Urgency creation: -10-30%

## Talent Management

### Attrition Rate

**Cálculo:**
```
Attrition = Departures / Average Headcount × 100%
```

**Benchmarks:**

**Consulting (junior):**
- Typical: 15-25% annually
- MBB: 20-30% (up-or-out)

**Consulting (senior):**
- Typical: 5-15%

**Legal:**
- Associates: 15-25%
- Partners: <5%

**Accounting:**
- Staff: 20-30% (high burnout)
- Partners: <5%

**Cost of attrition:**
- Replacement cost: 1.5-2× annual salary
- Lost productivity: 6-12 meses
- Client relationships: Varies

**Slide tipo:**
**Título:** "Attrition de 18% (vs 23% setor) driven por comp increases e flex work, saving $3M replacement costs annually"

**Partner admission rates:**

## Service Line Economics

### Strategy vs Implementation

**Strategy:**
- Higher rates ($400-1,000/h)
- Lower leverage (4:1)
- Higher gross margin (55-65%)
- Smaller teams, shorter engagements

**Implementation:**
- Lower rates ($150-400/h)
- Higher leverage (10:1)
- Lower gross margin (35-50%)
- Larger teams, longer engagements

**Blended model:** Cross-sell strategy → implementation

### Fixed-Fee vs Time & Materials

**Fixed-fee:**
- Predictable client cost
- Firm assumes scope risk
- Higher rates (20-40% premium if scoped well)
- Requires excellent scoping

**Time & Materials:**
- Client assumes scope risk
- Lower risk for firm
- Can lead to "meter running" perception

**Hybrid (capped T&M):**
- Best of both
- Ceiling protects client
- Firm shares upside if efficient

## Terminologia Especializada

**Billable hours:** Hours charged to clients
**Non-billable:** Internal time (training, BD, admin)
**Utilization:** Billable hours / available hours
**Realization:** Revenue collected / standard billing
**Write-down:** Reducing bill before sending
**Write-off:** Forgiving uncollected AR
**Blended rate:** Weighted average rate per team
**Standard rate:** Published hourly rate (pre-discount)
**Leverage:** Ratio of junior to senior staff
**Pyramid:** Staff structure (many junior, few senior)
**Up-or-out:** Promote or exit policy
**Rainmaker:** Partner who brings in clients
**Origination credit:** Who sourced the client
**Relationship credit:** Who manages the client
**Secondment:** Placing consultant at client site
**Retainer:** Monthly fee for ongoing access
**Success fee:** Contingent on outcome (M&A, litigation)
**Alternative fee arrangement (AFA):** Non-hourly billing
**Scope creep:** Expanding work beyond original agreement
**Value pricing:** Pricing based on outcome value, not hours
**RPE:** Revenue per employee
**PPP:** Profit per partner
**Lock-up:** Working capital tied in WIP + AR
**WIP:** Work in progress (unbilled)
**AR:** Accounts receivable (billed, not collected)
**DSO:** Days sales outstanding
**Pipeline:** Potential future engagements
**Coverage ratio:** Pipeline / target revenue
**Win rate:** % of opportunities won
**Whitespace:** Opportunity for additional services
**Cross-sell:** Selling other services to existing client
**Upsell:** Expanding current engagement
